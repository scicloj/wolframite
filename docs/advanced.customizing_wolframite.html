11  Customizing Wolframite

(ns advanced.customizing-wolframite

There are several ways to customize Wolframite to your liking.

11.0.1 Custom aliases

We’ve discussed Wolframite’s built-in aliases in Section 3.2.7. However, Wolframite allows you to individually tailor the user experience at the level of initialization:

(wl/restart! {:aliases '{🔋 Power}})
{:status :ok, :wolfram-version 14.2, :started? true}
(wl/! '(🔋 2 5))
32

, and function call,

(wl/restart!)
{:status :ok, :wolfram-version 14.2, :started? true}
(wl/! '(🔋🔋 2 5) {:aliases '{🔋🔋 Power}})
32

Use it how you want to!

TIP: You can also get convenience vars for your aliases in wolframite.wolfram by running something like (wolframite.impl.wolfram-syms.write-ns/write-ns! <path> {:aliases '{🔋 Power}}). After you load the file, you’ll be able to use (wl/! (w/🔋 2 5) {:aliases '{🔋 Power}}).
